Phase 1 Development Plan
1️⃣ AI Combat Fixes

✔ Ensure AI correctly engages enemies and does not stay passive.
✔ Improve AI targeting & reaction times.
✔ AI should not get stuck in hold fire mode.
✔ AI should track enemy movement instead of standing still.
2️⃣ AI Reinforcement Calls

✔ AI calls for helicopter support immediately upon engagement.
✔ Ground reinforcements arrive at a realistic distance.
✔ Reinforcements scale with convoy difficulty.
✔ If convoy is in trouble, more reinforcements may be called.
3️⃣ AI Response to Stolen Vehicles

✔ If a player steals an AI convoy vehicle, AI will:

    Call in air support.
    Helicopter will track the stolen vehicle.
    Drop AI troops onto the player.
    AI will pursue for a period before disengaging.

4️⃣ Debugging & Logging for AI

✔ Enable debug markers to track AI movement & engagements.
✔ Log AI decisions in server console for testing.
✔ Track stolen vehicle status (when AI starts pursuit & when they disengage).
